Privacy Policy

Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Ticker has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. We do not collect your personal information, and we do not run servers that receive it. What you enter stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Ticker is made by Jamie Watkins. You can reach us at callans.creations1@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

These are saved only on your device, and we never receive them:

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter one (used to greet you in the app)
  • The companies you follow, your level, lesson progress, streak, XP, badges and collected words
  • Your settings, and the alerts the app creates for companies you follow
  • Recently loaded prices, company details and headlines, so the app can show them when you are offline

Deleting the app, or choosing Settings → Start over, removes this information.

What the app sends to other companies

To show live market data, the Ticker app asks Ticker's data service for it. That is a small server run by Jamie Watkins on Cloudflare. It passes your request to the market-data providers we use for prices, charts and headlines, adds the access key so that no key is stored on your phone, and sends the answer back.

  • What is sent: stock ticker symbols (for the companies you follow or look up) and any words you type into search. Nothing else.
  • What is not sent: your name, your progress, or any account, because there are none.
  • We do not keep logs of requests and we do not build profiles. The data service keeps shared copies of market data (prices, company details, headlines) so it can answer everyone from one lookup. Those copies are the same for everyone and are not tied to you.

As with any internet service, the data service, Cloudflare and the market-data providers can see your device's IP address while handling a request. How they treat it is covered by their own privacy policies on their websites.

When you tap a news headline, it opens in your browser on the publisher's website, which has its own privacy practices.

Ask Ticker and Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, Ask Ticker uses Apple's on-device language model. Your questions are processed on your iPhone and are not sent to us or to any other server by Ticker.

Notifications and widgets

Alerts are created on your iPhone from market data and shown as local notifications. We do not use a push-notification server, and no notification token is collected. Widgets read the same data the app saved on your device, and refresh prices through the same data service the app uses.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal information
  • No user accounts, passwords or payment information. The app is free.

Children

Ticker is made for learning and is suitable for a general audience. Because we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect any from children.

Your choices

You can turn off notifications in the iPhone Settings app, change or remove your name in Ticker's Settings, and delete everything the app stores by choosing Start over or deleting the app. Because we do not hold your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Not financial advice

Ticker is an educational tool. Nothing in the app or on this website is investment, financial, tax or legal advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything. Prices can be delayed or wrong. Past moves do not predict the future.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will say so clearly in the app's update notes.
