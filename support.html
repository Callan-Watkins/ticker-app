Common questions

Why did the prices stop moving?

US stock markets are open 9:30 AM to 4 PM Eastern, Monday to Friday. When they are closed, you see the latest closing prices. Pull down on Home to refresh.

Why is there no chart on a stock page?

Charts need a data connection. If you are offline or the chart provider is busy, you will see today's low-to-high range instead. Try again in a minute.

I do not see the Ask tab.

Ask Ticker uses Apple Intelligence, which runs on your iPhone. It appears only on iPhones that support it. If your iPhone supports it, turn on Apple Intelligence in the iPhone Settings app and reopen Ticker.

How do alerts work?

Open Settings → Alerts to choose price moves and earnings reminders, and to allow notifications. Alerts are created on your iPhone while Ticker is open, and occasionally in the background when iOS allows it, so they are not instant.

How do I change my name or level?

Open the Settings tab. Your name is at the top, and your level is under Learning.

How do I reset the app?

Settings → About → Start over. This clears your name, followed companies, XP, streak and words on this device.

Can I buy or sell stocks in Ticker?

No. Ticker is only for learning and watching. It is not connected to any brokerage and never tells you what to buy or sell.

Where does the data come from?

Prices, company details and headlines come from live market-data providers. Data can be delayed or contain errors.
